## 🎙️ 6-Slide Speaker Script for Newsroom Roleplay

- **Anchor Opening:** 
  > *"Good evening, viewers. Welcome to Prime Time Mohalla on The Daily Thela Chronicle. Tonight's top story: India's 10 million unorganized street vendors are taking on Silicon Valley dark-store giants. Introducing WEkreta — the AI-powered phygital bridge connecting street thelas to online consumers."*
- **Flash Highlights:**

---

### Slide 2: The Ground Reality (Investigation & Problem)
- **Investigative Correspondent:**
  > *"Reporting live from the streets of Old Delhi: 10 million vendors drive 60% of urban fresh perishables, but face three crippling bottlenecks:*
  > 1. *The Digital Literacy Wall: 80%+ vendors cannot type SKU catalogs into complex vendor apps.*
  > 2. *The Invisibility Trap: Customers miss nearby vendors because street thelas move without live GPS.*
  > 3. *The Q-Commerce Squeeze: Dark stores charge 25-30% platform cuts, locking out small vendors.*
  > *WEkreta's mission: Zero typing, 2-tap ambient commerce."*

---

### Slide 3: Tech Blueprint (System Architecture)
- **Tech Desk Anchor:**
  > *"Taking a look under the hood at our 4-layer zero-cost architecture:*
  > - *Layer 1 (Client): Next.js mobile PWA + WhatsApp Cloud API Bot.*
  > - *Layer 2 (Ingestion): Continuous SpeechRecognition loop with auto-restart.*
  > - *Layer 3 (Intelligence): Google Gemini 2.5 Flash parsing spoken Hinglish to JSON + 30-min Mohalla Batching.*
  > - *Layer 4 (Storage): Neon Serverless PostgreSQL with Prisma ORM.*
  > *Our Whiteboard Defense: Zero paid mapping dependencies — Leaflet saves 94% platform OPEX over Google Maps ($0 vs $580/1k loads)!"*

---

### Slide 4: Live Field Demo (Innovation in Action)
- **Field Reporter:**

  > - *Innovation 3: 30-minute Mohalla batching room grouping orders within a 1.5 km geofence with 1-tap WhatsApp acceptance."*
